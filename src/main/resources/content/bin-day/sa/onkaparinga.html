Where this schedule comes from

Onkaparinga's schedule is read from the council's own service at the moment you ask for it, not from a copy we took earlier. That is why this page has no calendar printed on it: there is nothing here to go out of date.

Every council we cover is listed with the document or service its schedule came from on our coverage and data sources page.

About 183,000 people live in Onkaparinga. All of them are covered here - the lookup answers for any address the council collects from, not just the main town.
