Where this schedule comes from

Frankston's schedule is read from the council's own service at the moment you ask for it, not from a copy we took earlier. That is why this page has no calendar printed on it: there is nothing here to go out of date.

The source is connect.pozi.com. Every council we cover is listed the same way, with the document or service each schedule came from, on our coverage and data sources page.

About 145,000 people live in Frankston. All of them are covered here - the lookup answers for any address the council collects from, not just the main town.
