For councils

Your residents already ask us when their bins go out. We'd love to get every answer right, together.

Bin Space tells Australians which bins go out and when - with evening-before reminders - built from the schedules councils publish. We cover most of the country today, and every answer links back to the council data it came from.

Why work with us

  • Fewer missed-bin calls. Residents who get a reminder the evening before put the right bin out on the right day.
  • Accuracy you control. When you tell us about a calendar change before it lands, your residents never see a wrong day.
  • Your data stays yours. We read what you publish and we say so - the coverage page names the source for every council we answer for.

The single most useful thing you can do

Publish your collection data somewhere we can read it. In practice two files are enough for us to answer every address in your council:

  • Your zone or route boundaries as GeoJSON, KML or a shapefile, with the zone name in a field - not just drawn on a map image.
  • A dated calendar per zone - which weeks are which, for actual dates, not just "week A/week B". A machine-readable file is gold; even a clearly dated PDF works.

If you already have an address lookup on your website, that works too - tell us where it lives. And if your area has streets or localities with quirks (split services, rural runs, two-day streets), a street list settles what a boundary can't.

Want more than accurate data?

Some councils want a bin schedule and reminders they can point residents at as the official answer - on their own website, under their own name. That's a conversation we'd genuinely like to have. Tell us what your residents ask for and we'll show you what we could do together.

Get in touch

Email support@bin-space.app with Council partnership in the subject, tell us which council you're from, and point us at whatever you have - a file, a link, or just a question. A real person reads every message, usually within one business day.
